# About Dribble

Dribble is a Division I college basketball coaching sim. You take a job, set the rotation, recruit, and coach the games.

Career mode starts at a mid or low major. Pick a school to jump in anywhere, including a school you add yourself. Added schools play the schedule, the standings, and Selection Day like any other Division I program.

The gym, the news, the résumé, and the bracket use the same field once Selection Day locks.

It is free. No ads, no energy, no loot boxes. An optional tip lives in Settings.

The player page is [About](/about.html).
