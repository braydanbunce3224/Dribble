Dribble privacy policy

Last updated September 20, 2026.

Dribble is a single-player college basketball dynasty game. There is no account, no cloud save, and no sign-in.

What we collect

Nothing from you. Dynasty files, tutorial flags, name packs, and settings stay on this device (local storage / app storage). We do not run ads, analytics, crash reporters, or trackers in the game.

What we do not do

We do not sell data. We do not share data with third parties. We do not track you across apps or websites. We do not use your data to advertise. We do not collect precise location.

Children

Dribble is a sports simulation. It is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child provided personal information, delete the app to wipe local saves.

Names and schools

Schools, leagues, and boards in the game are original facsimile names unless you load a name pack. They are not affiliated with any university, conference, or media company.

Contact

If you contact support, we only see what you send. Delete the app, or clear site data, to wipe local saves.
